Privacy Policy for Dino Pet

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Dino Pet is a game for all ages, published by Nex FS Ltd. Young children play it, so this page is written with parents and carers in mind. Questions about it can be sent to admin@n3x.fun.

The short version

  • The game does not ask for a name, an email address, a photo, a location or anything else about the player.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no links to social media and nothing to buy with real money.
  • We, the publisher, receive no information about players at all.
  • The Android app shows one small advertisement banner. It treats every player as a child: the ads are not chosen from what a player has done.

What the game saves

The dinosaur and the player's progress are saved so the game can carry on next time. That save holds only game progress, including the name the player gives the dinosaur. It is kept:

  • in the Android app, in the app's own storage on the phone or tablet;
  • on YouTube, by YouTube, with the YouTube account in use;
  • on CrazyGames, by CrazyGames;
  • on other websites, in the browser's storage on the device.

We cannot see it. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in the browser, removes it.

Advertising in the Android app

The Android app shows one small banner advertisement in a strip above the game. It never covers the game. The only other advertisement is one a player chooses: when the dinosaur is asleep, the player can watch a video advertisement to wake it rested. It never plays unless the player taps to watch it. Both are supplied by Google AdMob.

The app does not ask anyone's age. It tells Google to treat every player as a child. Because of that:

  • ads are limited to those Google rates as suitable for everyone;
  • ads are not personalised: they are not chosen from a player's interests or from what the player has done in this or any other app;
  • the app does not have permission to read the device's advertising ID.

To deliver an advertisement at all, Google's software in the app receives some technical information, such as the device's internet (IP) address, which shows roughly where the device is, the kind of device and its system version and language, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google uses this to deliver and count ads and to prevent fraud. Google explains its practices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in its Privacy Policy.

Tapping an advertisement opens the advertiser's page outside the game. We suggest that young children play with a parent or carer nearby.

Websites that host the game

When the game is played on YouTube, Poki, CrazyGames, itch.io or another website, the game itself still collects nothing. The website's own privacy policy covers what that website collects, including any advertising it shows around the game. On Poki and CrazyGames the site may show a video advertisement as the dinosaur falls asleep. On CrazyGames the game leaves a place under its shop and album screens where CrazyGames shows a banner advertisement of its own.

Children

Dino Pet is for everyone, and children under 13 are among its players. We do not collect, keep or share personal information from children or from anyone else who plays. A parent or carer with a question or a concern can write to us at the email address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
